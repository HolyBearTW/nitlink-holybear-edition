nitlink-lockup.svg preserves the original vector asset.
nitlink-lockup.png is a transparent 176px-high rasterization for the native
GDI+ caption renderer, generated with @resvg/resvg-js 2.6.2. It is embedded
in NitLink.exe. The converter is a development tool and is not distributed.
